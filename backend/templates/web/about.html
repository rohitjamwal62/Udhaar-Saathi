{% trans "About LenDen Saathi" %}

{% trans "LenDen Saathi helps small shops in India keep honest accounts of udhaar (credit): what was sold, what was paid and what is still due." %}

{% trans "What is LenDen Saathi?" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}LenDen Saathi (लेनदेन साथी), also written Lenden Saathi or Len Den Saathi, is a digital khata and sales tracker. A shopkeeper records udhaar and payments on an Android phone, even without internet, and the customer can see and confirm the same entry.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What does the name mean?" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %}LenDen (लेन-देन) is the Hindi word for give and take: the money that goes out and comes back. Saathi (साथी) means companion. LenDen Saathi is the companion for every give-and-take in your shop.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Who is it for?" %}

  • {% trans "Kirana and general stores that sell on credit." %}
  • {% trans "Medical, hardware, cloth and other small shops with regular customers." %}
  • {% trans "Customers who want to see, in one place, what they owe each shop and what they have already paid." %}

{% trans "What it is not" %}

{% trans "LenDen Saathi is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, a lender or a debt-collection agency, and it does not give or collect loans." %}
