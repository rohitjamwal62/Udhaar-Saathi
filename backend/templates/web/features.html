Digital khata for shopkeepers

Keep your whole credit ledger in one place and always know who owes what.

  • Add customers and record goods sold on credit, itemised or as a total.
  • Record full or partial payments.
  • See total receivable and each customer's balance.
  • Attach bill photos to entries.
  • Send reminders, view reports and export your records.

Offline-first, sync once

Shops do not always have internet. The app saves every entry on the phone and syncs it exactly once when you are back online.

  • Add entries and payments without a connection.
  • No duplicates when the connection drops and returns.
  • Messages, one-time codes and confirmation links need internet.

Honest on both sides

Customers get a verified account that can link to many shops, but only after the customer approves each link.

  • Customers see their own entries, the balance per shop and the combined balance.
  • A customer can dispute a wrong entry.
  • A shopkeeper can never mark an entry as confirmed.
  • Corrections are reversals or adjustments with a reason. Never silent edits or deletes.
  • Amounts are exact. They are stored in paise, so there are no rounding errors.
  • Everything has an audit trail.

Confirmations are an acknowledgement and evidence trail, not guaranteed legal proof.

Sales tracking at the counter

Know what you sold today, this month or any period you choose.

Quick or itemised sales

Type one total for a fast sale, or list items when you need detail.

Split payments

Take part in cash, part by UPI and part by card in the same sale.

Credit sales linked to the khata

A sale on credit goes straight into the customer's khata.

Discounts, returns and refunds

Record them properly so your numbers stay right.

Daily, monthly and custom reports

Pick any date range to see your sales.

No double counting

When a customer pays an old due, it is a collection, not a new sale. It is not counted as sales.
