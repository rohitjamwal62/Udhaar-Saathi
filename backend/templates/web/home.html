How it works

Three simple steps

1

Add your customer

Enter a name and number. Your customer can link their own account later, only if they approve.

2

Record the udhaar

Write what was sold and the amount, or just the total. Add payments as they come.

3

Customer confirms

They see the entry and confirm it, or raise a dispute. You both see the same history.

Offline-first

No signal in the shop? Keep working.

Every entry is saved on the phone straight away. When internet returns, it is sent to the server exactly once, so nothing is lost and nothing is counted twice.

Sending messages, one-time codes and confirmation links needs internet.

Two-sided transparency

One entry. Two views. No surprises.

Your customer has their own verified account and can see their own entries, per shop and combined. A shopkeeper can never mark an entry as confirmed. Only the customer can.

  • Every entry shows its status: Shopkeeper recorded, Awaiting confirmation, Customer confirmed or Disputed.
  • Mistakes are fixed with a reversal and a reason. The audit trail stays.

A confirmation is an acknowledgement and evidence trail. It is not guaranteed legal proof.
