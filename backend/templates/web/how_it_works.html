How it works

Simple for the shopkeeper. Clear for the customer.

Both sides see the same record, so there is less to argue about.

If you run the shop

  1. Create your free accountVerify your email with a one-time code.
  2. Add customersA name and number is enough to begin.
  3. Record what you soldItemised or total only. Attach a bill photo if you like.
  4. Note each paymentFull or partial. The balance updates for you.
  5. Remind and reviewSend a reminder, check reports and export your records.

If you are a customer

  1. Create a verified accountOne account works for many shops.
  2. Approve the shop linkA shop sees your entries only after you approve.
  3. See your entriesView each shop's balance and your combined balance.
  4. Confirm or disputeAgree to an entry, or dispute one that is wrong.
