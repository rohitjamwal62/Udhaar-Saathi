Security

How we look after your records

No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise. Here is what we do.

  • HTTPS. Data moves between your phone, this website and our servers over an encrypted connection.
  • Hashed passwords. We never store your password as plain text.
  • One-time codes. They expire and can be used only once.
  • Rate limiting. Repeated attempts are slowed down to make guessing harder.
  • Data isolation. Each shop's data is kept separate from every other shop.
  • Private storage. Bills and documents are kept in private storage, not on public links.
  • Audit logs. Changes are recorded so there is a trail.
